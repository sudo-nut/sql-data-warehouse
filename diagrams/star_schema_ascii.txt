Relationships:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• fact_streams.song_id    →  dim_song.song_id      (Many-to-One)
• fact_streams.date_id    →  dim_date.date_id      (Many-to-One)
• fact_streams.market_id  →  dim_market.market_id  (Many-to-One)
• dim_song.artist_id      →  dim_artist.artist_id  (Many-to-One)

This is a typical star schema: 
- ONE central fact table (fact_streams) at the center
- FOUR dimension tables (dim_song, dim_date, dim_market, dim_artist) around it
- Simple, denormalized structure optimized for analytical queries
- Efficient JOIN performance with indexed foreign keys
